## What This Is

This repository is a public record of platform security failures. It documents the gap between what platforms claim to enforce and what they actually enforce.

Ban evasion goes unchecked. Policies go unenforced. Support goes silent. Users are left defenseless against coordinated abuse, harassment, and criminal activity that the platform's own rules prohibit.

This archive exists to hold platforms accountable to their own rules through public, sourced, verifiable documentation.

---

## Why It Exists

Platforms write policies they do not enforce. They promise safety they do not deliver. They bury reports, ignore appeals, and let bad actors return under new accounts while victims are left with nowhere to turn.

This is not a problem of one bad user. This is systemic negligence.

When a platform publicly prohibits ban evasion and then fails to ban an account that publicly admits to ban evasion, that is not an oversight. That is a broken system.

This archive documents that broken system, one case at a time.

## What Gets Documented

- Ban evasion admissions (public, timestamped)
- Enforcement failures (reports ignored, appeals buried)
- Support silence (no response, no resolution)
- Policy violations left unaddressed (documented with sources)
- Systemic patterns across multiple cases (correlated, not isolated)

Everything documented here meets a standard:

- **Verifiable claims only** — every assertion has a linked, publicly accessible source
- **Timestamps** — screenshots include capture dates and archived links where possible
- **Separation of verified and alleged** — clear distinction between what is confirmed and what is claimed
- **No private individuals** — public handles only
- **No doxing** — no addresses, employers, family details, or private identifiers

If a claim cannot meet these standards, it does not belong in this archive.
